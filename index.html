About Chapter

Driving Tech Excellence at MITS

ACM Mits Student Chapter is a premier student technology community at Madhav Institute of Technology and Science, Gwalior. We foster computing innovation, peer learning, and real-world problem solving.

Welcome to ACM Mits

Dedicated to advancing computing as a science and a profession. We bridge the gap between academic theory and industry engineering through hands-on technical workshops, competitive programming, hackathons, and collaborative software projects.

Developer First

Focus on code, architecture & practical skills.

Inclusive Community

Open for all engineering disciplines.

Our Mission

Build, Collaborate & Learn

"To build a community where students learn, collaborate and create meaningful technology-driven solutions."

Our Vision

Inspire Tech Innovators

"To inspire the next generation of developers, problem solvers and technology innovators."
